Custom Branding Techniques Guide

Not sure which print finish suits your logo? Explore our in-house branding options

Request Free Digital Mockup →
Apparel & Bags
Screen Printing

Vibrant, durable ink pressed through mesh stencils. Best for bold spot-colour logos on t-shirts, tote bags, and gazebos.

Ideal for: 25+ units • Up to 6 spot colours
Corporate Wear
Custom Embroidery

High-stitch-count thread branding for a textured, executive finish that outlasts repeated washing.

Ideal for: Golf shirts, jackets, caps & workwear
Metal & Wood
Laser Engraving

Precision fibre & CO2 laser etching that permanently reveals the underlying metal or wood grain without fading.

Ideal for: Vacuum flasks, metal pens, keyrings & bamboo
Full Colour
Digital & Sublimation

Photographic full-colour CMYK prints and heat-infused dyes for gradients, complex logos, and edge-to-edge lanyards.

Ideal for: Ceramic mugs, lanyards, notebooks & tech
Curved Items
Pad Printing

Flexible silicone pad transfers crisp, fine-detail logos onto curved, cylindrical, or uneven promotional surfaces.

Ideal for: Plastic pens, stress balls, power banks & bottles
Executive Gifts
Debossing & Foiling

Heated metal die presses your logo deep into leatherette or PU covers—available blind or with metallic gold/silver foil.

Ideal for: A5 notebooks, folders, diaries & wallets
